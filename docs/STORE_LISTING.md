# 随身工具箱 / Pocket Toolbox

## 简体中文

**一句话介绍**：二维码与条形码、个税和快捷计算、换算、计时与文档扫描，常用工具随手可得。

**应用介绍**：随身工具箱将十四类日常功能放在一个简洁应用中。你可以生成、保存、分享二维码及 Code 128、EAN-13、EAN-8 条形码，使用系统扫码界面扫描相机画面或相册图片；计算折扣、百分比与账单均摊；估算工资个税、稿酬、离职补偿、劳务报酬及特许权使用费的预扣税额；换算八类常用单位；创建多组倒计时或使用秒表；在设备支持时开启手电筒和水平仪；计算日期（含农历、年/月/日差值、年龄、工作日与 ISO 周数）、转换时间戳、整理文本、随机抽取；使用安全随机数生成密码；通过系统文档扫描控件拍摄或选择图片，生成 JPG 或 PDF 后主动导出。**全部功能离线可用，应用不申请网络权限、不发起任何网络请求，也不收集或上传使用数据**；节假日日历随应用内置，随新版本更新。二维码历史和计时器只保存在本机，可随时删除。个税估算不是税务申报或年度汇算结果。

**关键词建议**：二维码，条形码，个税计算，工资计算，折扣计算，均摊计算，文档扫描

## English

**Short description**: QR and barcodes, income tax estimates, document scanning and everyday tools that work offline.

**Description**: Pocket Toolbox brings fourteen everyday utilities together in one app. Create, save, share and scan QR codes and common barcodes; estimate resident individual income tax withholding for wages, manuscripts, severance, service income and royalties; calculate discounts, percentages and exact bill splits; convert units; run countdowns and a stopwatch; calculate dates (including lunar dates, interval, age, working days and ISO week numbers), convert timestamps, clean up text, and pick random numbers or list items. Generate passwords with secure random bytes. Scan paper or gallery images with the system document scanner and export JPG or PDF files. Use the flashlight and spirit level on supported devices. **Everything works offline: the app requests no network permission, makes no network requests and collects or uploads no usage data**; the holiday calendar is bundled and refreshed with new app versions. QR history and timers stay on your device and can be deleted at any time. Tax estimates are not filing or annual settlement results.

**Suggested keywords**: QR code, barcode, income tax, wage calculator, discount calculator, document scanner, offline tools

## 版本更新说明 / Release notes

**中文（v1.0.0 首个上架版本）**

- 新增：日期工具支持农历（干支、生肖、闰月）、工作日（排除法定节假日并计入调休上班日）、按天/工作日/周/月/年推算、倒数日；新增「时间戳」工具（秒/毫秒与本地、UTC 文本互转）。
- 新增：二维码页可直接查看本机历史记录。
- 改进：日期与时间改用系统选择器；输入框、结果卡与列表统一接入系统沉浸材质；工具卡片与详情页标题随中英切换即时更新。
- 隐私：应用不再申请网络权限，节假日日历随包内置，全部功能离线可用、不收集任何数据。

**English (v1.0.0, first release)**

- New: lunar dates (sexagenary cycle, zodiac, leap months), working-day counts that skip public holidays and include make-up workdays, date offset by days/working days/weeks/months/years, and a countdown line; a new Timestamp tool (seconds or milliseconds to local and UTC text and back).
- New: the QR page can open the on-device history directly.
- Improved: system date and time pickers; immersive material on inputs, result cards and list rows; tool card titles and detail titles now follow the language switch immediately.
- Privacy: the app requests no network permission, the holiday calendar ships with the app, and every feature works offline without collecting data.
