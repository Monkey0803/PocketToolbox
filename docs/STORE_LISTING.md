# 随身工具箱 / Pocket Toolbox

## 简体中文

**一句话介绍**：二维码与条形码、个税和快捷计算、换算、计时与文档扫描，常用工具随手可得。

**应用介绍**：随身工具箱将十三类日常功能放在一个简洁的离线应用中。你可以生成、保存、分享二维码及 Code 128、EAN-13、EAN-8 条形码，使用系统扫码界面扫描相机画面或相册图片；计算折扣、百分比与账单均摊；估算工资个税、稿酬、离职补偿、劳务报酬及特许权使用费的预扣税额；换算八类常用单位；创建多组倒计时或使用秒表；在设备支持时开启手电筒和水平仪；计算日期、整理文本、随机抽取；使用安全随机数生成密码；通过系统文档扫描控件拍摄或选择图片，生成 JPG 或 PDF 后主动导出。二维码历史和计时器只保存在本机，可随时删除。个税估算不是税务申报或年度汇算结果。

**关键词建议**：二维码，条形码，个税计算，工资计算，折扣计算，均摊计算，文档扫描

## English

**Short description**: QR and barcodes, income tax estimates, document scanning and everyday offline tools.

**Description**: Pocket Toolbox brings thirteen everyday utilities together in an offline app. Create, save, share and scan QR codes and common barcodes; estimate resident individual income tax withholding for wages, manuscripts, severance, service income and royalties; calculate discounts, percentages and exact bill splits; convert units; run countdowns and a stopwatch; calculate dates; clean up text; and pick random numbers or list items. Generate passwords with secure random bytes. Scan paper or gallery images with the system document scanner and export JPG or PDF files. Use the flashlight and spirit level on supported devices. QR history and timers stay on your device and can be deleted at any time. Tax estimates are not filing or annual settlement results.

**Suggested keywords**: QR code, barcode, income tax, wage calculator, discount calculator, document scanner, offline tools
