# 随身工具箱 / Pocket Toolbox

## 简体中文

**一句话介绍**：二维码与条形码、个税和快捷计算、换算、计时与文档扫描，常用工具随手可得。

**应用介绍**：随身工具箱将多类日常功能放在一个简洁应用中。你可以生成、保存、分享二维码（文本、网址、WiFi、名片、电话、短信、邮件、位置八种内容模板）及 Code 128、EAN-13、EAN-8 条形码，使用系统扫码界面扫描相机画面或相册图片；计算折扣（含立减与折扣反推）、百分比（含增减与变化）与账单均摊，也可按加价率定价或算毛利率；估算工资个税、稿酬、离职补偿、劳务报酬及特许权使用费的预扣税额；换算十四类常用单位（长度、重量、温度、面积、体积、速度、时间、数据容量、压力、能量、功率、角度、频率、油耗，含斤两尺寸里亩等市制单位），可切换显示精度并一眼看到同类别其他单位；创建多组倒计时或使用秒表；在设备支持时开启手电筒（可调亮度，除了常亮还能发国际求救信号 SOS 等求援灯语、任意字母数字的摩尔斯灯语和 1–5 Hz 频闪，可设定时关闭与屏幕常亮），也可把屏幕当作柔光板做屏幕补光，并使用水平仪；计算日期（含农历、年/月/日差值、年龄、工作日与 ISO 周数）、转换时间戳、整理文本（去空行、去重、排序、大小写、词与段落统计）、随机抽取；使用安全随机数生成密码；通过系统文档扫描控件拍摄或选择图片，生成 JPG 或 PDF 后主动导出。工具网格支持按中英文名搜索，收藏只需点一下星标。**全部功能离线可用，应用不申请网络权限、不发起任何网络请求，也不收集或上传使用数据**；节假日日历随应用内置，随新版本更新。二维码历史和计时器只保存在本机，可随时删除；单位换算只在本机记住上次使用的类别、单位与精度。个税估算不是税务申报或年度汇算结果。

**关键词建议**：二维码，条形码，个税计算，工资计算，折扣计算，均摊计算，单位换算，工具箱，文档扫描

## English

**Short description**: QR and barcodes, income tax estimates, document scanning and everyday tools that work offline.

**Description**: Pocket Toolbox brings eighteen everyday utilities together in one app. Create, save, share and scan QR codes — text, URL, WiFi, contact card, phone, SMS, email or location, with a choice of error-correction level and colour — plus twelve barcode symbologies; read codes from a picture in your gallery; estimate resident individual income tax withholding for wages, manuscripts, severance, service income and royalties; calculate discounts (with instant discounts and reverse lookups), percentages and exact bill splits, or work out a markup and margin; estimate loan repayments with equal instalments or equal principal; convert fourteen categories of units (length, mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data size, pressure, energy, power, angle, frequency and fuel economy, including Chinese market units) with a selectable precision and a panel showing every other unit in the category; run countdowns (quick presets, repeat mode) and a stopwatch with lap times; calculate dates (including lunar dates, interval, age, working days and ISO week numbers), convert timestamps, clean up text (blank lines, duplicates, sorting, letter case, word and paragraph counts) plus Base64/URL encoding and MD5/SHA-1/SHA-256 digests, and pick random numbers, roll dice, flip a coin, shuffle or group a list. Generate passwords with secure random bytes. Scan paper or gallery images with the system document scanner and export JPG or PDF files. Use the flashlight on supported devices (adjustable brightness, plus rescue signals such as international SOS, Morse light for any letters or digits, and a 1–5 Hz strobe, with auto-off and keep-screen-on) or turn the screen into a soft light panel, and use the spirit level. Search the tool grid by Chinese or English name and favourite a tool with one tap. **Everything works offline: the app requests no network permission, makes no network requests and collects or uploads no usage data**; the holiday calendar is bundled and refreshed with new app versions. QR history, timers, stopwatch laps and the unified history (QR creations and scans plus copied text, conversion, timestamp and calculation results) stay on your device and can be deleted at any time; unit conversion only remembers your last category, units and precision on this device. Tax estimates are not filing or annual settlement results.

**Suggested keywords**: QR code, barcode, income tax, wage calculator, discount calculator, unit converter, document scanner, offline tools

## 版本更新说明 / Release notes

**中文（v1.0.0 首个上架版本）**

- 新增：折扣与均摊扩展到五种计算——折扣计算（原价 × 折数，再减立减金额，给出折后价、节省与等效折扣）、折扣反推（由折后价与折数求原价，或由两个价格求折数）、百分比计算（求百分之几、求占比、增减百分比、百分比变化）、加价与毛利（按加价率定价，或由成本与售价同时给出毛利率与加价率）、账单均摊（总金额、人数、小费，余下的分自动分配）；每种结果都能一键复制，复制结果同时记入本机历史（新类别「计算结果」）。

- 新增：日期工具支持农历（干支、生肖、闰月）、工作日（排除法定节假日并计入调休上班日）、按天/工作日/周/月/年推算、倒数日；新增「时间戳」工具（秒/毫秒与本地、UTC 文本互转）。
- 新增：二维码页可直接查看本机历史记录。
- 新增：工具网格支持按中英文名搜索（含空结果提示）；二维码支持文本、网址、WiFi、名片、电话、短信、邮件、位置八种内容模板。
- 新增：单位换算扩展到十四类（含市制斤两尺寸里亩与压力、能量、功率、角度、频率、油耗），可切换 6/8/10/12 位有效数字，并列出同类别其他单位；本机记住上次的类别、单位与精度。
- 新增：文本工具扩充为三段共十一个操作（去空行/去首尾空格/压缩空白/合并为一行、行去重/忽略大小写去重/只看重复行/行倒序/升序/降序、大小写与首字母大写）与六项统计（字符、非空白字符、行数、单词、中文字、段落）；另加编码与摘要：Base64、URL 编解码与 MD5/SHA-1/SHA-256（全部本机计算）。
- 新增：随机选择支持骰子（1–5 颗）、抛硬币、名单随机排序与分组；计时支持常用时长预设、重复计时与秒表计次（含最快/最慢标记）。
- 新增：条形码支持十二种码制（Code 128、Code 39、Code 93、EAN-13、EAN-8、UPC-A、UPC-E、ITF-14、Codabar，以及 Data Matrix、PDF417、Aztec 三种二维码制），带校验位校验与逐码制输入提示。
- 新增：收藏卡片长按可「置顶」或直接取消收藏（收藏顺序即显示顺序），也可进入排序模式拖拽调整顺序。
- 新增：时间戳工具支持按 18 个常用时区换算，显示该时区时间、UTC 偏移与「比本机快/慢」，偏移由系统按该时刻给出（含夏令时）。
- 新增：二维码可设容错等级（L/M/Q/H）与码色（经典黑白/深蓝/墨绿）；二维码页与条形码页都能**从相册图片识别**码（截图里的码也能读出来，纯本机识别）。
- 新增：贷款计算工具——等额本息与等额本金，给出月供/首末月月供、每月递减、首月利息、总利息与总还款（本机估算，实际以银行为准）。
- 新增：颜色工具——`#RGB`/`#RRGGBB`/`rgb()` 与 HEX/RGB/HSL 互转，并检查前景背景的 WCAG 对比度（普通/大号文字的 AAA/AA 判定与建议前景色）。
- 新增：深色模式——界面颜色改为资源化，**跟随系统深色/浅色自动切换**（含卡片、文字、描边、下拉与页签）。
- 新增：**亲戚称呼（亲戚关系计算器）**——输入或点选一串亲属关系（如「爸爸的哥哥」「father's older brother」），给出称谓、关系链、辈分与性别；常见关系（祖辈、父母的兄弟姐妹及配偶、堂表兄弟姐妹、侄甥、岳父母/公婆、配偶的兄弟姐妹等）直接给名词，远亲与姻亲给关系描述，长幼有歧义的关系给并列称谓与提示。全部在本机计算，输入不保存、不联网、不申请权限。
- 新增：健康计算——按中国成人标准给出 BMI 与分类、该身高下的正常体重范围，并按 Mifflin-St Jeor 估算基础代谢（BMR）与每日消耗（TDEE，五档活动量）。结果为估算，不构成医疗建议。
- 新增：工具搜索支持关键词——`BMI`、`房贷`、`hex`、`抽签`、`农历` 这类别名/缩写都能搜到对应工具。
- 新增：科学计算器——一行表达式求值，支持括号、`^` 幂（右结合）、`mod` 取模、`sin/cos/tan/asin/acos/atan/ln/log/sqrt/abs` 与常量 `pi`/`e`，三角函数可切 DEG/RAD，非法表达式给出具体原因。
- 新增：界面语言支持**跟随系统**——语言下拉为「跟随系统 / 简体中文 / English」三项，默认跟随系统（取系统或应用当前语言，取不到时用中文）；原有选择仍然有效。
- 新增：设置页显示**设备信息**（设备型号、系统版本、API 版本、应用版本、设备类型），只在本机读取、不写存储、不联网、不申请权限。
- 新增：指南针工具——读取系统方向传感器显示方位角（一位小数）与十六方位名称，盘面随方位旋转、顶部三角指示手机顶部所指方向；倾斜时提示放平、读数漂移时提示画「8」字校准。只在该页面打开时读取，不保存读数、不申请权限。
- 新增：桌面长按应用图标可用的 4 个快捷方式（生成二维码 / 生成条形码 / 科学计算器 / 计时与秒表），点击直达对应工具页。
- 新增：桌面**服务卡片「常用工具」**——长按图标选「卡片」即可添加 2×2 / 2×4 / 4×4 三种尺寸；格子内容收藏优先（没有收藏时是二维码、单位换算、计时、折扣与均摊），点一格直达对应工具页；改收藏或切语言后卡片自动更新。卡片只读本机收藏与语言偏好，不申请权限、不联网。
- 新增：统一历史记录——除了二维码生成与扫码，文本、换算与时间戳的复制结果也会记入本机历史，可按类别筛选并一键清空（全部保存在本机、可随时删除）。
- 新增：历史记录支持关键字搜索、删除当前筛选结果，点击条目可直接跳回对应工具并回填内容。
- 新增：收藏卡片长按可「置顶」或直接取消收藏（收藏顺序即显示顺序）。
- 修复：收藏「时间戳」后重启应用不再丢失；收藏页按收藏顺序显示（最近收藏在前）。
- 修复：秒表点开始/计次后界面不刷新（仅在其它动画同时运行时才刷新）。
- 改进：日期与时间改用系统选择器；输入框、结果卡与列表统一接入系统沉浸材质；工具卡片与详情页标题随中英切换即时更新。
- 隐私：应用不再申请网络权限，节假日日历随包内置，全部功能离线可用、不收集任何数据。

**English (v1.0.0, first release)**

- New: lunar dates (sexagenary cycle, zodiac, leap months), working-day counts that skip public holidays and include make-up workdays, date offset by days/working days/weeks/months/years, and a countdown line; a new Timestamp tool (seconds or milliseconds to local and UTC text and back).
- New: the QR page can open the on-device history directly.
- New: search the tool grid by Chinese or English name (with an empty-state hint); QR codes now support text, URL, WiFi, contact card, phone, SMS, email and location templates.
- New: unit conversion grew to fourteen categories (including Chinese market units plus pressure, energy, power, angle, frequency and fuel economy), with 6/8/10/12 significant digits and a panel listing every other unit in the category; the category, units and precision are remembered on this device.
- New: text tools now offer eleven operations in three groups (blank lines / trim / collapse spaces / one line, unique / case-insensitive unique / duplicates only / reverse / sort A-Z / sort Z-A, upper / lower / title case) and six counts (characters, non-space, lines, words, CJK, paragraphs), with use-as-input and clear actions; plus encoding and digests — Base64, URL encode/decode and MD5/SHA-1/SHA-256, all computed on device.
- New: the random picker adds dice (1–5), a coin flip, list shuffling and grouping; timers add quick duration presets, a repeat mode, and stopwatch laps with fastest/slowest markers.
- New: the discount & split tool grew to five calculators — discount (price × factor, minus an instant discount, showing the final price, the saving and the equivalent discount), reverse discount (recover the original price from the final price and factor, or work out the factor from two prices), percentage (a percentage of a number, a part as a percentage, an increase/decrease, and the change between two values), markup and margin (price from a markup rate, or margin and markup from cost and price), and bill splitting (amount, people, tip, with leftover cents assigned). Every result has a copy button, and copied results are kept on device under the new "Calculation" history category.
- New: unified history — besides QR creations and scans, copied text, conversion and timestamp results are kept on device, filterable by category and clearable in one tap.
- New: barcodes in twelve symbologies (Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, ITF-14, Codabar, plus Data Matrix, PDF417 and Aztec 2D codes) with check-digit validation and per-format input hints.
- New: history search by keyword, delete the current filtered results, and tap a record to jump back to the tool with the content filled in.
- New: long-press a favourite card to move it to the top or remove it, or switch on sort mode and drag cards into your own order.
- New: the Timestamp tool converts to 18 common time zones, showing the zone time, the UTC offset and how it compares with local time; offsets come from the system for that instant, daylight saving included.
- New: QR codes take an error-correction level (L/M/Q/H) and a colour preset (classic, deep blue, deep green); both the QR and barcode pages can read a code straight from a picture in your gallery.
- New: a Loan calculator — equal instalments or equal principal, with the monthly payment, first/last payment, monthly decrease, first-month interest, total interest and total repayment (calculated on device; your bank's rounding may differ).
- New: a Colour tool — #RGB/#RRGGBB/rgb() and HEX/RGB/HSL conversion, plus a WCAG contrast check for foreground and background (AAA/AA verdicts for body and large text, with a suggested foreground).
- New: dark mode — the interface palette now lives in resources and follows the system light/dark setting automatically (cards, text, borders, menus and the tab bar).
- New: a **Kinship calculator** — type or tap a chain of relationships (e.g. “father's older brother”) to see how to address that person, plus the relationship chain, the generation gap and the gender. Common relations (grandparents, aunts and uncles with their spouses, cousins, nephews and nieces, in-laws) get a single term; distant and in-law relations are described instead, and relations whose age order is ambiguous get both terms with a note. Everything is computed on device: the input is never saved, and the feature adds no permission and no network access.
- New: a Health calculator — BMI with Chinese adult cut-offs, the normal weight range for your height, and Mifflin-St Jeor estimates of your basal metabolic rate (BMR) and daily energy (TDEE) across five activity levels. Estimates only, not medical advice.
- New: keyword search for tools — aliases and abbreviations such as BMI, mortgage, hex, dice or lunar calendar now find the right tool.
- New: a Scientific calculator — evaluate a single-line expression with parentheses, `^` power (right-associative), `mod`, `sin/cos/tan/asin/acos/atan/ln/log/sqrt/abs` and the constants `pi`/`e`; trigonometry switches between degrees and radians, and invalid input reports the exact reason.
- New: the interface language can **follow the system** — the language menu now offers Follow system / 简体中文 / English, defaulting to following the system (your system or app language, falling back to Chinese); your previous choice keeps working.
- New: the Settings tab shows device information (model, system version, API level, app version, device type). It is read on-device only — nothing is stored or uploaded, and no permission is required.
- New: a Compass tool — reads the system direction sensor to show the bearing (one decimal place) and the 16-point name, with a rose that rotates as you turn and a fixed marker showing where the top of the device points; it hints when the device is tilted and suggests the figure-8 calibration when the reading drifts. The sensor is read only while the page is open, no reading is stored, and no permission is required.
- New: four home-screen shortcuts (Create a QR code / Create a barcode / Scientific calculator / Timers and stopwatch) that open the matching tool directly.
- Fixed: favouriting the Timestamp tool no longer disappears after a restart, and the favourites page now lists tools in the order you added them (newest first).
- Fixed: the stopwatch did not refresh after pressing start or lap unless another animation happened to be running.
