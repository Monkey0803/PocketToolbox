# 随身工具箱 / Pocket Toolbox

## 简体中文

**一句话介绍**：31 个常用工具——二维码与条形码、图片压缩与证件照、个税与金额大写、折扣与换算、进制与文本对比、URL 编解码、纪念日与计时（含番茄钟）、世界时钟、网络工具与网络查询（IP 归属地 / 域名 WHOIS / TLS 证书），离线可用、联网只在显式触发时发生。

**应用介绍**：随身工具箱将多类日常功能放在一个简洁应用中。你可以生成、保存、分享二维码（文本、网址、WiFi、名片、电话、短信、邮件、位置八种内容模板，可设容错等级、**自定义码色**——手输 `#RRGGBB` 或用系统取色色轮取色，背景固定纯白，太浅的颜色会被拦下，还可从相册选一张图片做**居中 Logo**）及 Code 128、EAN-13、EAN-8 条形码，使用系统扫码界面扫描相机画面或相册图片；计算折扣（含立减与折扣反推）、百分比（含增减与变化）与账单均摊，也可按加价率定价或算毛利率；估算工资个税（**含年终奖单独计税**，本机估算）、稿酬、离职补偿、劳务报酬及特许权使用费的预扣税额；换算常用单位（长度、重量、温度、面积、体积、速度、时间、数据容量、压力、能量、功率、角度、频率、油耗，含斤两尺寸里亩等市制单位，**以及货币类别**），可切换显示精度并一眼看到同类别其他单位；创建多组倒计时或使用秒表；在设备支持时开启手电筒（可调亮度，除了常亮还能发国际求救信号 SOS 等求援灯语、任意字母数字的**摩尔斯编解码**灯语和 1–5 Hz 频闪，可设定时关闭与屏幕常亮），也可把屏幕当作柔光板做屏幕补光，并使用水平仪；计算日期（含农历、年/月/日差值、**时间点差值**、年龄、工作日与 ISO 周数）、转换时间戳、对比两段文本的差异、整理文本（去空行、去重、排序、大小写、词与段落统计）、**URL 编解码（组件 / 整串网址 / 表单 / 自定义保留字符四种方式，同一屏同时给出编码与解码结果，并把网址里的查询参数逐条拆开）**、随机抽取；记录**纪念日**（名称 + 日期 + 是否每年重复，最多 20 条，每条还可开启**本地提醒**——到当天 09:00 由系统本地通知提醒，不需要联网）；做**进制与位运算**换算；生成密码（可选「密码 / PIN 码」并给出强度预估；可关闭「排除易混字符」、一次生成 1/2/3/5/10 条并逐条复制；还可用「强度检查」在本机内存里查看已有密码的长度、字符类别与常见弱口令特征，不落盘、不查泄露库）；使用**网络工具**做域名解析、端口连通探测、查看 HTTP 状态与响应头，以及完全离线的网址分解，也可以读取网页链接信息（含短链展开）；用**网络查询**查 IP 归属地与域名 WHOIS（公开 RDAP 服务），或读取某个主机（可带端口）的 **TLS 证书**——主题、颁发者、有效期、SAN、协议版本与密码套件，并如实区分「系统信任校验通过 / 未通过 / 未校验」；通过系统文档扫描控件拍摄或选择图片，生成 JPG 或 PDF 后主动导出，并可**在本机提取图片里的文字**（端侧识别、不联网；识别结果可复制或直接送进文本工具），还能逐页删除、继续追加扫描、把 JPG 存进相册；扫描的默认滤镜与拍摄方式可设置。工具网格支持按中英文名搜索，收藏只需点一下星标。**在别的应用里分享文本或链接，可直接送到本应用生成二维码或用文本工具处理**；倒计时结束、水平仪到位、指南针对准锁定的方位时会有轻振提示（可在设置页关闭）。**离线工具完整可用；联网动作全部由你显式触发、可在设置页「联网」区块逐项关闭（含总开关与「仅 Wi-Fi」），也不收集或上传使用数据**（当前为：网络工具与链接信息、网络查询（IP 归属地与域名 WHOIS、TLS 证书）、汇率更新、节假日数据更新、节气与农历节日数据包更新、应用内更新检查；启动、后台、桌面卡片与分享扩展都不联网）；节假日日历与节气/农历节日数据随应用内置，也可在设置页主动检查更新。二维码历史和计时器只保存在本机，可随时删除；纪念日清单与汇率缓存同样保存在本机，可逐条删除或在更新时覆盖；单位换算只在本机记住上次使用的类别、单位与精度。**外观可选 7 套主题色（经典蓝 / 青碧 / 松绿 / 紫罗兰 / 玫红 / 靛蓝 / 石墨），也可用系统取色面板挑任意强调色**——只换按钮、图标与选中态的颜色，浅色与深色各自适配，颜色会自动夹取到可读的对比度区间。个税估算不是税务申报或年度汇算结果，年终奖单独计税为按现行政策的本机估算。

## English

**Short description**: QR and barcodes, income tax estimates, document scanning and everyday tools that work offline.

**Description**: Pocket Toolbox brings thirty-one everyday utilities together in one app. Create, save, share and scan QR codes — text, URL, WiFi, contact card, phone, SMS, email or location, with a choice of error-correction level, a custom code colour (typed or picked with the system colour wheel, always on a pure white background) and an optional centre logo composited on device — plus twelve barcode symbologies; read codes from a picture in your gallery; estimate resident individual income tax withholding for wages, manuscripts, severance, service income and royalties, including separate taxation of the year-end bonus (estimated on device); calculate discounts (with instant discounts and reverse lookups), percentages and exact bill splits, or work out a markup and margin; estimate loan repayments with equal instalments or equal principal; convert fourteen categories of units (length, mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data size, pressure, energy, power, angle, frequency and fuel economy, including Chinese market units) plus a Currency category with a selectable precision and a panel showing every other unit in the category; run countdowns (quick presets, repeat mode) and a stopwatch with lap times; calculate dates (including lunar dates, interval, age, working days and ISO week numbers) and the difference between two times of day, convert timestamps, compare two pieces of text and see their differences, convert between bases and do bitwise operations, clean up text (blank lines, duplicates, sorting, letter case, word and paragraph counts) plus Base64/URL encoding, MD5/SHA-1/SHA-256 digests, and a dedicated URL codec (component / full URL / form / custom modes, showing the encoded and decoded result side by side and breaking query parameters out one per line), and pick random numbers, roll dice, flip a coin, shuffle or group a list. Generate passwords with secure random bytes, as either a password or a PIN, with a strength estimate; look-alike characters can be allowed, one to ten passwords can be generated at once with a copy button each, and a Strength check reads an existing password in memory on the device to show its length, character classes and common weak-password patterns — it stores nothing and queries no breach database. Scan paper or gallery images with the system document scanner and export JPG or PDF files. Use the flashlight on supported devices (adjustable brightness, plus rescue signals such as international SOS, Morse light for any letters or digits, and a 1–5 Hz strobe, with auto-off and keep-screen-on) or turn the screen into a soft light panel, and use the spirit level, and run Network tools for DNS lookups, TCP port checks, HTTP status and response headers, plus a fully offline URL-parts breakdown and link info (including short-link expansion); look up IP information and domain WHOIS through the public RDAP service, or read a host's TLS certificate — subject, issuer, validity, SANs, protocol version and cipher suite — with the system trust result reported honestly as passed, failed or not checked. Record anniversaries (name + date + whether it repeats yearly, up to 20, each with an optional **local reminder** at 09:00 on the day itself, delivered by a system local notification and needing no network). Search the tool grid by Chinese or English name and favourite a tool with one tap. **Text or links shared from another app can be sent straight to the QR code or text tools**, and a short vibration marks a finished countdown, a level surface and a compass aligned with a locked bearing (it can be turned off in Settings). **Offline tools remain fully usable. Every network action is user-triggered and can be switched off individually in Settings → Networking (including a master switch and a Wi-Fi-only option), and the app collects and uploads no usage data** — currently: Network tools and link info, IP lookup and domain WHOIS, TLS certificate reading, rate updates, holiday data updates, the solar-term data pack and the in-app update check; app start-up, the background, the home-screen widget and the share extension never go online. The holiday calendar and the solar-term/festival data ship with the app and can also be updated from Settings. QR history, timers, stopwatch laps and the unified history (QR creations and scans plus copied text, conversion, timestamp and calculation results) stay on your device and can be deleted at any time; unit conversion only remembers your last category, units and precision on this device. This version adds an image tool (compress, resize, rotate/flip and convert between JPEG/PNG/WebP, all in on-device memory), amount in words, an ID-card and bank-card checker that keeps numbers in memory only, a world clock for up to 12 cities, and a Pomodoro mode plus a weighted prize wheel. Tax estimates are not filing or annual settlement results.
